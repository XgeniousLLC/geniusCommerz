Admin Panel Overview

Key Features

  • Dual Authentication: Separate admin and customer login systems
  • Role-Based Access Control: Customize permissions per team member
  • Real-Time Analytics: Track revenue, orders, and customer metrics
  • Multi-Language Support: English, Bengali, and more

  • Courier Integration: Pathao, RedX, Steadfast shipping
  • Payment Gateways: Stripe, bKash, Nagad, Cash on Delivery

Login & Account Access

Default Administrator Account

Field Value
Email Address admin@geniuscommerz.com
Password password
Login URL https://yourdomain.com/admin/login
Security Notice: Change your password immediately after first login. Use a strong, unique password with at least 12 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

Change Your Password

  1. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner
  2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu
  3. Click the "Change Password" button
  4. Enter your current password for verification
  5. Enter your new password (minimum 12 characters)
  6. Confirm the new password by entering it again
  7. Click "Update Password" to save changes

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

  1. Go to your Profile settings
  2. Navigate to the Security section
  3. Click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication"
  4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator)
  5. Enter the 6-digit code displayed in the app
  6. Save your backup codes in a secure location
  7. Two-factor authentication is now enabled

Product Management

Accessing Products

  1. Click "Catalog" in the sidebar
  2. Select "Products" from the menu

Products List

The products page displays all your store's products with various management options:

  • Search by product name, SKU, or category
  • Filter by status (Active/Draft), category, brand, or price range
  • Sort by name, price, stock quantity, or creation date
  • Bulk actions to change status or delete multiple products
  • Export product list as CSV or Excel file

Add New Product

  1. Click the "Add Product" button in the top-right corner
  2. Complete the product form (see sections below)
  3. Click "Save & Publish" to make it live or "Save as Draft"

Product Information Form

Order Management

Accessing Orders

  1. Click "Orders" in the sidebar

Orders List

View and manage all customer orders:

  • View orders by status: All, Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled
  • Search by order number, customer name, or email
  • Filter by date range
  • Sort by date, amount, or status
  • Bulk export orders as CSV

Order Status Workflow

Status Description Next Action
Pending Order received, awaiting confirmation Review and confirm
Confirmed Order verified and ready to process Prepare for shipping
Processing Items being picked and packed Dispatch to courier
Shipped Package with courier in transit Wait for delivery
Delivered Order received by customer Request product review
Cancelled Order cancelled by customer or admin Process refund if applicable

Payment Configuration

Accessing Payment Settings

  1. Click "Settings" in the sidebar
  2. Click "Payment" tab

Available Payment Methods

Cash on Delivery (COD)

  • Enabled by default, no setup required
  • Payment collected at time of delivery
  • Good option for building customer trust initially

Stripe - International Credit and Debit Cards

Setup instructions:

  1. Register account at stripe.com
  2. Get your Publishable Key and Secret Key
  3. Go to Settings > Payment tab
  4. Toggle "Enable Stripe"
  5. Paste your Publishable Key in the appropriate field
  6. Paste your Secret Key in the appropriate field
  7. Click "Save Settings"
  8. Always test with Stripe test keys first
Important: Test with test keys (pk_test_...) before switching to live keys (pk_live_...). Live keys will process real charges.
